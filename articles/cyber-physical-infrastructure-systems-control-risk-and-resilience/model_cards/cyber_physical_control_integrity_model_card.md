# Cyber-Physical Control Integrity Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative signal-quality, telemetry-reliability, dependency-intensity, control-validation, human-oversight, control-integrity, and resilience indicators
- flag cyber-physical loops, assets, domains, dependencies, and controls requiring governance review
- support reproducible cyber-physical infrastructure examples

## Not intended for

- certified control engineering
- production infrastructure operations
- public safety automation
- regulatory reporting without institutional review
- cybersecurity assurance
- replacing operator authority, domain engineering, field testing, or public governance

## Required caveats

Sample data and thresholds are illustrative. Real deployment requires certified engineering, safety review, cybersecurity assessment, operational authority, domain expertise, operator training, field validation, and accountable governance.
