# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before digital infrastructure is used for critical services, public digital infrastructure, emergency communication, cloud migration, infrastructure monitoring, operational analytics, identity systems, cross-agency data exchange, public-service delivery, or resilience planning, it should pass a readiness gate.

1. Service objectives, critical users, decision uses, valid-use limits, prohibited uses, and responsible owners are defined.
2. Coverage, latency, bandwidth, uptime, affordability, accessibility, and support needs are documented.
3. Cloud regions, data centers, edge nodes, backup, storage, portability, recovery, and capacity constraints are documented.
4. APIs, schemas, standards, registries, data agreements, and integration tests are documented.
5. Authentication, authorization, consent, auditability, privacy safeguards, and exclusion risks are reviewed.
6. Security controls, logging, incident response, backup, failover, degraded-mode operation, and recovery are tested.
7. Critical dependencies, vendor concentration, portability, exit strategies, and procurement obligations are documented.
8. Assumptions, limitations, risks, outage procedures, rights impacts, service claims, and review cycles are documented.
