# Digital Infrastructure Resilience Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative access, network capacity, compute/storage, interoperability, trust/security, dependency, exposure, inclusion, and resilience indicators
- flag service zones, public-service dependencies, cloud dependencies, interoperability gaps, inclusion risks, and continuity gaps requiring governance review
- support reproducible digital infrastructure examples

## Not intended for

- certified network engineering
- public procurement decisions
- cybersecurity assurance
- regulatory reporting without institutional review
- replacing accessibility testing, privacy review, competition analysis, or public governance

## Required caveats

Sample data and thresholds are illustrative. Real deployment requires engineering validation, public consultation, accessibility review, cybersecurity assessment, privacy and rights review, vendor and competition analysis, and accountable institutional governance.
