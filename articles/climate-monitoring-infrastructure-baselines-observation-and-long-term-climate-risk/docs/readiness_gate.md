# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before climate monitoring outputs are used for public reporting, infrastructure standards, climate-risk assessment, adaptation planning, mitigation tracking, or policy decision-making, they should pass a readiness gate.

1. Climate variables, domains, indicators, and decision uses are defined.
2. Observing assets, locations, ownership, operational status, and variables are documented.
3. Instrument, siting, calibration, maintenance, and processing metadata are available.
4. Missingness, station moves, instrument changes, and known breakpoints are reviewed.
5. Reference periods, baseline methods, and completeness thresholds are documented.
6. Datasets are versioned, preserved, and traceable to source observations.
7. Limitations, uncertainty, coverage gaps, and valid decision uses are stated.
8. Responsible institutions, update cycles, maintenance duties, and public access rules are defined.
