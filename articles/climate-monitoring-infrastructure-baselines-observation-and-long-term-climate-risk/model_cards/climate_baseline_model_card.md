# Climate Baseline and Anomaly Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative climate baselines
- compute anomalies against a documented reference period
- flag low-completeness or weak-metadata station records
- support reproducible climate monitoring examples

## Not intended for

- official climatological assessment
- attribution science
- operational climate services
- certified station-quality decisions
- replacing domain expert review

## Required caveats

- sample data are illustrative
- baseline periods must be chosen and documented carefully
- station metadata and calibration strongly affect interpretation
- real climate analysis requires uncertainty, homogeneity, and domain-specific review
