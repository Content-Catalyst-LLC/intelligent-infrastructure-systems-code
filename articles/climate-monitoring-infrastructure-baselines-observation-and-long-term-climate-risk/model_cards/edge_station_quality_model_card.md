# Edge Station Quality Model Card

## Intended use

- demonstrate edge validation for climate station telemetry
- detect out-of-range readings, low power, and basic quality failures
- compare embedded, MicroPython, and HDL validation patterns

## Not intended for

- official station certification
- replacing calibration programs
- replacing scientific quality-control pipelines

## Required caveats

- thresholds are illustrative
- field deployment requires calibration, maintenance, redundancy, metadata capture, and archive integration
