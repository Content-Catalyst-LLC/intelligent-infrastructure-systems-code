# Engineering Notes

## Design priorities

1. Define public service goals before selecting technology.
2. Preserve cross-domain context: transport, energy, water, stormwater, buildings, waste, environmental systems, digital infrastructure, emergency response, and civic services.
3. Measure service continuity, accessibility, inclusion, resilience, trust, and environmental burden.
4. Track data quality, latency, location validity, missingness, calibration, metadata, and provenance.
5. Evaluate privacy, surveillance risk, language access, disability access, offline service pathways, and digital divides.
6. Document cybersecurity, failover, manual operations, public communication, and incident response.
7. Connect analytics to maintenance, dispatch, emergency response, public service improvement, planning, regulation, and investment.

## Common failure modes

- dashboards without governance authority
- digital services excluding offline, disabled, low-income, language-minority, or documentation-limited users
- sector-specific smart systems creating hidden cross-domain fragility
- proprietary platforms creating vendor lock-in
- real-time data used without quality flags
- public claims made without audit trails
- cybersecurity and failover treated as afterthoughts
