# Smart City Infrastructure Systems Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Public purpose | What public outcomes should smart infrastructure improve? | Smart city objective manifest |
| Physical infrastructure | Which transport, energy, water, buildings, waste, environmental, civic, and digital assets are involved? | Urban infrastructure inventory |
| Urban observability | What service, environmental, asset, telemetry, and governance signals are observed? | Urban observability records |
| Cross-domain dependency | Which infrastructure domains depend on one another for continuity? | Dependency edge list |
| Public value | Does the system improve service continuity, accessibility, resilience, inclusion, and trust? | Public-value indicator catalog |
| Rights and inclusion | Are privacy, language access, disability access, offline access, and digital divides assessed? | Digital inclusion and rights review |
| Cybersecurity and resilience | Can systems operate safely under outage, attack, platform failure, or degraded data conditions? | Cybersecurity and fallback plan |
| Response and governance | Are indicators connected to action, maintenance, public communication, investment, or policy? | Governance and response log |
