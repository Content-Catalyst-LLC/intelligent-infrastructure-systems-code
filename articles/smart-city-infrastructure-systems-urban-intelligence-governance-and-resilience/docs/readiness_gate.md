# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before smart city infrastructure systems are used for public reporting, operations, digital services, infrastructure prioritization, emergency management, environmental exposure analysis, service automation, or resilience planning, they should pass a readiness gate.

1. Public outcomes, service domains, users, decision pathways, and valid-use limits are defined.
2. Physical assets, service zones, digital systems, agencies, and critical dependencies are documented.
3. Metadata, latency, missingness, calibration, provenance, APIs, schemas, and shared identifiers are tracked.
4. Privacy, accessibility, digital inclusion, language access, offline pathways, and public legitimacy are assessed.
5. Authentication, segmentation, logging, incident response, failover, manual fallback, and platform continuity are defined.
6. Alerts, indicators, and model outputs are connected to maintenance, public communication, service improvement, emergency response, or planning action.
7. Metrics, assumptions, limitations, responsible agencies, review cycles, and public claims are documented.
8. Incidents, failures, complaints, service gaps, and public feedback lead to revised systems and governance.
