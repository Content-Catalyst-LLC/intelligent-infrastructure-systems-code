# Mathematical Lens

## Citywide observability

\[
O_{\mathrm{city}} =
\sum_{d=1}^{n}
w_d
\left(
\alpha Q_d +
\beta C_d +
\gamma I_d +
\delta S_d +
\eta G_d
\right)
\]

Citywide observability depends on the quality, coverage, interoperability, service relevance, and governance capacity of each infrastructure domain.

## Coordination knowledge

\[
K_{\mathrm{coord}} =
\frac{E_{\mathrm{documented}}}{E_{\mathrm{critical}}}
\]

Coordination knowledge compares documented cross-domain dependencies with the critical dependencies that must be understood for service continuity.

## Urban risk

\[
R_{z,t} =
H_{z,t}
\times
E_z
\times
V_z
\times
(1 - G_z)
\]

Urban risk rises when hazard intensity, exposure, and vulnerability are high and governance response capacity is weak.

## Service continuity

\[
C_{\mathrm{service},d,t} =
\frac{S_{d,t}}{S_{d,\mathrm{normal}}}
\]

Service continuity compares service available during disruption with expected normal service capacity.

## Public urban value

\[
P_{\mathrm{urban}} =
\lambda_1 C_{\mathrm{service}} +
\lambda_2 A +
\lambda_3 R_{\mathrm{resilience}} +
\lambda_4 U_{\mathrm{inclusion}} +
\lambda_5 T_{\mathrm{trust}}
-
\lambda_6 B_{\mathrm{burden}}
\]

Public urban value rises with service continuity, accessibility, resilience, inclusion, and trust, while unequal burden reduces overall performance.
