# Public Evidence Package Template

Required public-facing fields:

- smart city system name and scope
- public purpose and service domains
- infrastructure assets and service zones covered
- data sources, latency, missingness, and quality caveats
- service-continuity, accessibility, inclusion, resilience, and trust indicators
- privacy, rights, language access, disability access, and offline service safeguards
- cybersecurity, platform continuity, and fallback procedures
- responsible agencies, vendors, operators, and governance authority
- public reporting schedule and review cycle
- limitations and non-operational caveats

Public-facing evidence should distinguish raw telemetry, quality-controlled records, indicators, model outputs, operational decisions, and public claims.
