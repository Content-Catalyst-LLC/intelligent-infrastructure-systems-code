# Smart City Public Value Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative service-continuity, observability, public-value, dependency-stress, digital-access, and privacy-risk indicators
- flag infrastructure domains requiring governance, operations, maintenance, inclusion, rights, or resilience review
- support reproducible smart city infrastructure examples

## Not intended for

- certified municipal operations
- public-safety automation
- emergency-management authority
- accessibility compliance determination
- privacy compliance determination
- cybersecurity certification
- replacement for public planning judgment

## Required caveats

Sample data and thresholds are illustrative. Real deployment requires validated infrastructure data, rights review, privacy review, accessibility review, cybersecurity testing, public authority, procurement review, community engagement, and governance approval.
