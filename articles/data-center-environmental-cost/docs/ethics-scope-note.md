# Ethics and Scope Note

Data centers support important public, scientific, economic, and social functions, but they also place demands on shared energy, water, land, and material systems.

Applied analysis should account for community impacts, ratepayer costs, regional water stress, environmental justice, land-use tradeoffs, grid reliability, and the social value of the workloads being scaled.

This scaffold uses synthetic data for educational purposes. It does not replace engineering studies, utility planning, permitting review, environmental impact assessment, public consultation, or regulatory oversight.
