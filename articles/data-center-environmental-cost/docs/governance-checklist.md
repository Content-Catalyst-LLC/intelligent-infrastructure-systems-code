# Data Center Environmental Governance Checklist

## Energy and grid

- What is the annual electricity demand?
- What is the peak load?
- Does the facility increase local or regional peak demand?
- Who pays for grid upgrades?
- Are renewable claims hourly, regional, and additional?

## Water

- What is the water source?
- How much water is withdrawn?
- How much is consumed?
- Is the facility in a water-stressed basin?
- How does cooling design affect seasonal water demand?

## Cooling

- What cooling technology is used?
- What is the seasonal PUE?
- What is the WUE?
- Are energy-water tradeoffs disclosed?

## Backup power

- What backup fuel or technology is used?
- What are local air-quality impacts?
- Are cleaner backup systems feasible?

## Supply chain

- What is the hardware refresh cycle?
- Are embodied impacts reported?
- Are reuse, repair, and recycling programs documented?

## Public accountability

- Are environmental claims independently verifiable?
- Are local communities consulted?
- Are tax incentives justified against public costs?
