# Validation Plan

A full applied data center environmental analysis should validate:

1. IT load, facility load, and peak demand from metered data.
2. Hourly electricity consumption and grid emissions intensity.
3. Renewable procurement claims, including additionality, region, and time matching.
4. Cooling technology, PUE, and seasonal thermal performance.
5. Water withdrawal, water consumption, source, discharge, and basin stress.
6. Backup-generator fuel use, testing frequency, and local air emissions.
7. Server and accelerator lifecycle, embodied impacts, reuse, and disposal.
8. Construction materials, embodied carbon, and land-use impacts.
9. Workload composition, including training, inference, storage, and networking.
10. Grid upgrade requirements and cost allocation.
11. Community impacts including water competition, noise, land use, and ratepayer exposure.
12. Public transparency and independent verification of environmental claims.

This scaffold is synthetic and educational. It is not an operational environmental-impact model.
