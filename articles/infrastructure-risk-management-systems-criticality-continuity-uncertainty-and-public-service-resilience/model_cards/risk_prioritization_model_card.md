# Risk Prioritization Model Card

## Intended use

- prioritize infrastructure risks across probability, consequence, criticality, dependency, mitigation effectiveness, continuity readiness, and governance readiness
- support review workflows and governance watchlists
- create reproducible article companion outputs

## Not intended for

- replacing engineering inspection
- certifying regulatory compliance
- automating public safety decisions
- justifying risk deferral without human review
- reducing public harm to a single score

## Required caveats

- scores are illustrative
- weights require local calibration
- high-consequence risks require domain expert review
- residual risk must be accepted explicitly by accountable owners
