# Risk Governance Model Card

## Intended use

- document risk ownership, escalation, residual-risk acceptance, public evidence, and review status
- ensure high-priority risks connect to accountable decisions
- support institutional learning and public transparency

## Not intended for

- replacing legal, regulatory, or emergency authority
- obscuring risk acceptance
- treating unresolved risks as closed because they were documented

## Required caveats

- governance records should identify owners and review cycles
- residual risks require explicit acceptance
- public evidence should explain assumptions and uncertainty
