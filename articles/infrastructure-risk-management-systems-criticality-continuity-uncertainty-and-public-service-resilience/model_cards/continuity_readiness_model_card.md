# Continuity Readiness Model Card

## Intended use

- evaluate fallback modes, recovery-time objectives, continuity exercises, and after-action review status
- identify high-criticality risks requiring continuity review
- connect risk management to essential service preservation

## Not intended for

- replacing emergency management planning
- certifying operational readiness without exercises
- hiding continuity gaps behind mitigation scores

## Required caveats

- continuity plans must be exercised
- recovery-time objectives must be realistic
- public-service continuity may require resources beyond technical controls
