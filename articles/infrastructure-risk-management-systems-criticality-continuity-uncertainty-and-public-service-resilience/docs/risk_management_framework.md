# Infrastructure Risk Management Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Asset and service layer | Which assets, systems, services, owners, users, and public functions are at risk? | Asset-service register |
| Hazard and vulnerability layer | Which physical, environmental, cyber, financial, supply-chain, institutional, and social risks exist? | Risk register |
| Criticality and dependency layer | Which functions matter most and how can failure propagate? | Criticality matrix and dependency graph |
| Assessment and prioritization layer | Which risks are most consequential after likelihood, criticality, controls, and uncertainty are considered? | Priority scoring workflow |
| Treatment and mitigation layer | Which controls, investments, redundancy, maintenance, adaptation, procurement, or finance actions reduce risk? | Treatment and mitigation plan |
| Continuity and recovery layer | Can essential services continue and recover under degraded conditions? | Continuity and recovery log |
| Risk finance layer | Which risks are reduced, retained, transferred, insured, or reserved for? | Risk-financing record |
| Governance layer | Who owns risk, accepts residual exposure, approves action, and communicates public evidence? | Risk governance log |
