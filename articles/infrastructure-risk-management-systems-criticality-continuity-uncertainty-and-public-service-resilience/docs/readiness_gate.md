# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before an infrastructure risk management system is used for investment prioritization, resilience planning, continuity review, cyber-physical coordination, insurance strategy, public reporting, emergency preparedness, or governance decisions, it should pass a readiness gate.

## Checks

1. System scope, decision use, public-service purpose, risk appetite, and valid-use limits are defined.
2. Critical assets, services, owners, users, and dependencies are documented.
3. Hazards, vulnerabilities, likelihood, consequence, uncertainty, controls, and residual risks are documented.
4. Essential functions, substitutes, dependency centrality, and recovery difficulty are assessed.
5. Climate, cyber, asset failure, fiscal, supply-chain, and cascading-risk scenarios are defined.
6. Mitigation, redundancy, maintenance, adaptation, procurement, financing, and control actions are linked to risks.
7. Risk reduction, retention, transfer, insurance, reserves, and recovery funding are documented.
8. Essential services have fallback modes, recovery-time objectives, and continuity plans.
9. Risk owners, escalation rules, residual-risk approvals, and public evidence processes are defined.
