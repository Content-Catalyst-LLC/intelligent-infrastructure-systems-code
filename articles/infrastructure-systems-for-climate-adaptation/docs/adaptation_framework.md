# Climate Adaptation Infrastructure Evidence Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Climate risk | Which hazards, baselines, scenarios, time horizons, and uncertainty ranges shape the decision? | Climate scenario manifest |
| Service continuity | Which service must continue, for whom, and under what stress? | Critical service registry |
| Asset exposure | Which assets, routes, facilities, and ecological systems are exposed? | Asset exposure inventory |
| Vulnerability | Which communities and services have lower adaptive capacity? | Vulnerability and equity screen |
| Intervention | Which hard, ecological, operational, governance, relocation, or demand measures are being compared? | Adaptation option register |
| Finance | How will the intervention be delivered, maintained, and renewed? | Implementation and finance plan |
| Governance | Who owns standards, monitoring, public caveats, and revisions? | Governance log |
