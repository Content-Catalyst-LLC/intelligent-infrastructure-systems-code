# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before infrastructure adaptation is used for public investment, resilience claims, land-use decisions, service planning, or vulnerable-population protection, it should pass a readiness gate.

## Checks

1. Climate scenario, time horizon, and uncertainty statement are documented.
2. Critical service and acceptable outage threshold are defined.
3. Asset exposure and infrastructure dependencies are mapped.
4. Vulnerable populations and adaptive-capacity gaps are included.
5. Adaptation options are compared, not assumed.
6. Maladaptation risks are screened.
7. Lifecycle finance and maintenance responsibilities are assigned.
8. Monitoring indicators and reporting cadence are defined.
