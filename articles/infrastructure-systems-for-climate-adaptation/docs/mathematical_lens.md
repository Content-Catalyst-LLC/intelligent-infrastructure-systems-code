# Mathematical Lens

## Climate risk

\[
R_{\mathrm{climate}} = H \times E \times V
\]

Climate risk increases when hazard intensity, exposure, and vulnerability reinforce one another.

## Effective vulnerability

\[
V_{\mathrm{effective}} = \frac{V}{1 + A}
\]

Adaptive capacity reduces effective vulnerability when institutions, infrastructure, social supports, finance, and operations can respond.

## Dependency-adjusted risk

\[
R_{\mathrm{dependency}} = R_{\mathrm{asset}} + \sum_i \alpha_i R_i
\]

Dependency-adjusted risk accounts for the fact that a water plant, pump station, rail corridor, cooling center, communications node, or hospital can inherit risk from power, transport, water, workforce, or digital dependencies.

## Service continuity

\[
S_{\mathrm{continuity}} = 1 - \frac{T_{\mathrm{outage}}}{T_{\mathrm{critical}}}
\]

Service continuity measures whether a critical infrastructure service remains available during the period in which failure would produce public harm.

## Adaptation readiness

\[
Q_{\mathrm{adaptation}} =
w_1C_{\mathrm{scenario}} +
w_2D_{\mathrm{dependency}} +
w_3P_{\mathrm{service}} +
w_4E_{\mathrm{equity}} +
w_5F_{\mathrm{finance}} +
w_6M_{\mathrm{maintenance}} +
w_7O_{\mathrm{observability}} +
w_8G_{\mathrm{governance}} -
w_9K_{\mathrm{maladaptation}}
\]

Adaptation readiness increases when scenario assumptions, dependency mapping, service protection, equity review, finance, maintenance, observability, and governance are strong; it decreases when maladaptation risk is high.
