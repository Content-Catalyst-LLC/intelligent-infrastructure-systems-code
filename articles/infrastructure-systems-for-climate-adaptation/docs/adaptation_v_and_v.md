# Verification and Validation

Verification asks whether the adaptation measure was implemented according to specification. Validation asks whether the measure actually supports the intended public service under plausible climate stress.

## Verification examples

- pump capacity matches design specification
- backup power operates during commissioning test
- permeable surface storage meets construction specification
- heat-reflective roof retrofit meets material and coverage requirements
- sensor telemetry is transmitted at required interval

## Validation examples

- drainage service remains within acceptable outage threshold during scenario exercise
- cooling center remains usable during compound heat and power stress
- vulnerable-population access improves rather than merely average coverage
- wetland buffer performs during observed rainfall events
- public reporting explains uncertainty and known limits
