# Smart Grid Infrastructure Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative grid observability, voltage adequacy, flexibility adequacy, balancing pressure, service continuity, cyber-physical risk, and resilience indicators
- flag grid assets and service zones requiring operational, maintenance, DER, cybersecurity, interoperability, governance, or resilience review
- support reproducible smart grid infrastructure examples

## Not intended for

- certified grid operations
- protection engineering automation
- reliability compliance determination
- market settlement
- public-safety automation
- cybersecurity certification
- replacement for operator, regulator, or planner judgment

## Required caveats

Sample data and thresholds are illustrative. Real deployment requires validated telemetry, topology models, certified operators, protection engineering, cybersecurity testing, reliability standards, regulatory governance, market rules, customer protections, field engineering, and institution-specific operating procedures.
