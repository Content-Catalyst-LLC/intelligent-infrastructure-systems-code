# Urban Sensor Observability Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative sensor quality, coverage gaps, and urban observability scores
- flag sensors or service zones requiring governance, maintenance, calibration, or coverage review
- support reproducible urban sensing examples

## Not intended for

- certified infrastructure operations
- emergency warning issuance
- cybersecurity certification
- privacy compliance review
- automated public-safety decisions

## Required caveats

Sample data and thresholds are illustrative. Real monitoring requires calibrated devices, secure communications, privacy review, cybersecurity testing, field validation, and public governance.
