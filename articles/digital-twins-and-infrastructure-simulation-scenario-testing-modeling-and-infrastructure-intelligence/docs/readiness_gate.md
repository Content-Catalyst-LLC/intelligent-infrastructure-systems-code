# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before a digital twin is used for operations, maintenance prioritization, investment planning, public communication, risk assessment, climate adaptation, emergency response, or governance decisions, it should pass a readiness gate.

## Checks

1. System scope, decision use, intended users, and valid-use limits are defined.
2. Data sources are documented, current, quality-checked, and linked to metadata.
3. Model assumptions, parameters, versions, and behavioral logic are documented.
4. Twin state estimation is traceable to data sources and model logic.
5. Scenarios define assumptions, stressors, interventions, time horizon, and uncertainty.
6. Models are validated against observed outcomes, benchmark cases, or expert review where possible.
7. Sensitivity testing has identified important assumptions and fragile outputs.
8. Interoperability requirements are documented across GIS, BIM, telemetry, asset systems, and work orders.
9. Cybersecurity and access-control requirements are reviewed.
10. Governance responsibilities are documented for ownership, updates, public communication, and decision review.
