# Digital Twin State Model Card

## Intended use

- estimate simplified asset condition, load, climate exposure, service criticality, and failure risk
- illustrate how a digital twin state table can organize current-state representation
- support scenario workflow examples

## Not intended for

- operational asset health certification
- real-time safety decisions
- direct regulatory reporting
- replacing inspections or telemetry validation

## Required caveats

- state values are synthetic
- failure risk is illustrative
- partial observability is expected
- field inspection and engineering review remain necessary
