# Public Evidence Model Card

## Intended use

- summarize digital twin scope, assumptions, validation, uncertainty, and governance status
- support transparent communication about simulation outputs
- prevent scenario outputs from being mistaken for authoritative predictions

## Not intended for

- hiding model limitations
- replacing public review
- presenting simulation results without caveats

## Required caveats

- the twin is a decision-support environment
- public claims require human review
- uncertainty and valid-use limits must accompany outputs
