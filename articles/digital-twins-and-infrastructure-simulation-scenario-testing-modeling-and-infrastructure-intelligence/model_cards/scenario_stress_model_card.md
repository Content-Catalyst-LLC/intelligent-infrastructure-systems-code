# Scenario Stress Model Card

## Intended use

- compare simplified scenario outcomes across load, climate, disruption, and intervention assumptions
- demonstrate how digital twins can support scenario testing and decision-value comparison
- produce educational outputs for infrastructure intelligence workflows

## Not intended for

- operational control
- capital approval
- public safety decisions
- emergency response without field validation
- replacing engineering analysis

## Required caveats

- scenario outputs are conditional analyses, not predictions
- stress multipliers are illustrative
- asset behavior is simplified
- network interdependence is not fully represented
- governance review is required before any decision use
