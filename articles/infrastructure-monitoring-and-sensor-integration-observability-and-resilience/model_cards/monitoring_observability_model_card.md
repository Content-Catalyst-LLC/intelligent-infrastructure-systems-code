# Infrastructure Monitoring Observability Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative signal-quality, calibration-confidence, telemetry-reliability, metadata-completeness, coverage, observability, actionability, and resilience indicators
- flag sensors, assets, service zones, and domains requiring calibration, telemetry, metadata, coverage, security, or governance review
- support reproducible monitoring and sensor-integration examples

## Not intended for

- certified infrastructure operations
- public safety automation
- regulatory reporting without institutional review
- cybersecurity assurance
- field calibration certification
- replacing domain engineering, inspection, or accountable response authority

## Required caveats

Sample data and thresholds are illustrative. Real deployment requires calibrated sensors, validated telemetry, field inspection, domain engineering, cybersecurity review, operational technology governance, regulatory compliance, public-agency review, and accountable institutional response.
