# Urban Resilience Service-Continuity Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative service-continuity scores
- identify recovery lag against target recovery times
- summarize urban risk using hazard, exposure, vulnerability, and governance response
- flag services where dependency stress, equity gaps, or weak continuity require review
- support reproducible urban resilience examples

## Not intended for

- official emergency management
- certified infrastructure design
- capital budget approval
- public-safety decision automation
- replacing community-led resilience planning

## Required caveats

- sample data are illustrative
- real resilience analysis requires validated hazard data, engineering review, public-sector authority, community engagement, and financial analysis
- service-continuity indicators should not be interpreted as citywide resilience ratings
