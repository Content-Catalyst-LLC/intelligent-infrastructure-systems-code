# Edge Service Status Model Card

## Intended use

- demonstrate edge validation for urban infrastructure service-status telemetry
- detect low service capacity, low battery, and backup-power gaps
- compare embedded, MicroPython, and HDL validation patterns

## Not intended for

- official infrastructure operations
- emergency warning issuance
- safety-critical automation
- replacing utility or emergency-management systems

## Required caveats

- thresholds are illustrative
- field deployment requires validated sensors, secure communications, fail-safe design, maintenance, public authority, and operations integration
