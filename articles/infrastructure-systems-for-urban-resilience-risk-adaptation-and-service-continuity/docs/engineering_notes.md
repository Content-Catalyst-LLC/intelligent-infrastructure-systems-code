# Engineering Notes

## Design priorities

Urban resilience infrastructure should be engineered around the full service-continuity chain:

1. Define critical services and continuity targets.
2. Map hazards, chronic stresses, exposed zones, and vulnerability.
3. Identify infrastructure dependencies and cascading failure pathways.
4. Evaluate asset condition, redundancy, backup systems, and recovery routes.
5. Score continuity, recovery lag, dependency stress, and equity gaps.
6. Connect findings to capital planning, maintenance, adaptation, and emergency management.
7. Use after-action reviews to revise design, governance, and investment priorities.

## Common failure modes

- resilience measured only at the asset level
- hidden dependencies across power, water, transport, communications, and health
- recovery targets not connected to operational capacity
- backup systems untested under compound scenarios
- citywide averages hiding neighborhood-level service collapse
- nature-based systems not maintained or protected
- maintenance backlogs treated separately from resilience risk
- recovery restoring pre-existing vulnerability
