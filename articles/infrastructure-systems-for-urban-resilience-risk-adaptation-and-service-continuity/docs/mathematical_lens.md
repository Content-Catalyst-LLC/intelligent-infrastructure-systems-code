# Mathematical Lens

## Urban risk

\[
R_{z,t} = H_{z,t} \times E_z \times V_z \times (1 - G_z)
\]

Urban risk rises when hazard intensity, exposure, and vulnerability are high and governance response capacity is weak.

## Service continuity

\[
C_{s,t} = \frac{S_{s,t}}{S_{s,\mathrm{normal}}}
\]

Service continuity compares service available during disruption with normal service capacity.

## Interdependency stress

\[
I_s = \sum_{j=1}^{n} D_{sj} \times F_j
\]

Interdependency stress depends on the strength of service dependencies and the failure state of connected systems.

## Recovery lag

\[
L_{\mathrm{recovery},s} =
\max(0,\ T_{\mathrm{recover},s} - T_{\mathrm{target},s})
\]

Recovery lag measures how far expected or observed recovery time exceeds the target recovery time.
