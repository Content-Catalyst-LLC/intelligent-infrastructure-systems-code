# Public Evidence Package Template

Required public-facing fields:

- urban resilience system name and scope
- hazards and chronic stresses covered
- critical services and continuity targets
- dependency assumptions and known cascading-risk pathways
- exposure, vulnerability, and equity caveats
- redundancy and recovery-time assumptions
- nature-based and spatial adaptation assets
- maintenance and asset-condition caveats
- governance owner and participating agencies
- public reporting schedule
- after-action review process
- review history

Public-facing evidence should distinguish asset robustness, service continuity, recovery capacity, adaptation capacity, and equity outcomes.
