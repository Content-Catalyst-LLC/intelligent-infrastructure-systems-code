# Urban Resilience Infrastructure Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Hazard and stress layer | Which shocks and chronic stresses affect the city? | Hazard and stress register |
| Critical-service layer | Which services must continue or recover quickly? | Critical-service inventory |
| Dependency layer | Which services depend on which other infrastructure systems? | Infrastructure dependency graph |
| Continuity and recovery layer | How much service remains during disruption, and how quickly can it recover? | Continuity and recovery plan |
| Vulnerability and equity layer | Who is exposed, who lacks service redundancy, and who recovers slowly? | Vulnerability and service-equity review |
| Ecological and spatial layer | Which nature-based or spatial systems reduce heat, flood, and exposure risk? | Nature-based resilience register |
| Governance and finance layer | Who funds, maintains, coordinates, learns, and adapts? | Governance and adaptation log |
