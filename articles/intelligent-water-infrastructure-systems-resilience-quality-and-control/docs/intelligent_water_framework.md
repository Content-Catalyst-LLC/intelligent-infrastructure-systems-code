# Intelligent Water Infrastructure Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Water-service objective | What service, quality, public-health, environmental, and resilience goals should the system support? | Water infrastructure objective manifest |
| Physical assets | Which sources, treatment works, pumps, tanks, reservoirs, valves, pipes, sewers, drains, meters, and sensors are included? | Water asset inventory |
| Sensing and sampling | Are pressure, flow, tank level, turbidity, chlorine, pH, conductivity, rainfall, wastewater, stormwater, and laboratory records captured? | Water telemetry and quality records |
| Hydraulic control | Are pressure adequacy, leakage, non-revenue water, tank levels, pump state, and valve behavior understood? | Leakage and hydraulic-control review |
| Public-health assurance | Are quality thresholds tied to sampling, laboratory evidence, compliance records, and public notification procedures? | Water-quality and public-health review |
| Wastewater and stormwater | Are inflow, infiltration, overflow risk, drainage capacity, rainfall, and flood exposure tracked? | Wastewater and stormwater risk review |
| Cyber-physical integrity | Are SCADA, telemetry, field devices, remote access, credentials, firmware, and fallback procedures protected? | Cyber-physical monitoring review |
| Governance and response | Are findings connected to treatment adjustment, field dispatch, work orders, public communication, or capital planning? | Governance and response log |
