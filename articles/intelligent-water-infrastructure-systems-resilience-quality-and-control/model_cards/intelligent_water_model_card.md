# Intelligent Water Infrastructure Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative quality compliance, pressure adequacy, leakage, service continuity, observability, overflow risk, and resilience indicators
- flag water assets and service zones requiring operational, public-health, maintenance, stormwater, wastewater, cybersecurity, or governance review
- support reproducible intelligent water infrastructure examples

## Not intended for

- certified utility operations
- water-quality compliance determination
- laboratory testing replacement
- public-health action without proper authority
- hydraulic engineering certification
- wastewater or stormwater permit compliance determination
- cybersecurity certification
- replacement for operator, regulator, or public-health judgment

## Required caveats

Sample data and thresholds are illustrative. Real deployment requires validated telemetry, calibrated sensors, laboratory testing, certified operators, hydraulic engineering, public-health authority, regulatory governance, cybersecurity testing, environmental review, community communication, and institution-specific operating procedures.
