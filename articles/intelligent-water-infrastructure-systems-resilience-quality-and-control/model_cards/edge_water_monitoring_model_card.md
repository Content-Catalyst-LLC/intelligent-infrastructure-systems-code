# Edge Water Monitoring Model Card

## Intended use

- demonstrate edge validation for water telemetry
- detect low pressure, turbidity, chlorine residual, pH, low power, stale telemetry, and fallback problems
- compare embedded, MicroPython, and HDL validation patterns

## Not intended for

- certified water-quality compliance
- public-health action automation
- treatment control without operator review
- replacing laboratory testing
- replacing cybersecurity or utility operations review
