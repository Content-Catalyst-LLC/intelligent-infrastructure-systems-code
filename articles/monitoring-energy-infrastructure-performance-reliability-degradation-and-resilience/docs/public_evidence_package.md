# Public Evidence Package Template

Required public-facing fields:

- energy monitoring system name and scope
- public/service purpose and decision uses
- asset classes, locations, owners, criticality, and service zones covered
- telemetry sources, latency, missingness, calibration, and quality caveats
- reliability, availability, degradation, power-quality, resilience, and restoration metrics
- maintenance, inspection, and capital-planning response pathways
- cybersecurity, access-control, telemetry continuity, and fallback safeguards
- responsible agencies, operators, vendors, and governance authority
- reporting schedule and review cycle
- limitations and non-operational caveats

Public-facing evidence should distinguish raw telemetry, quality-controlled records, indicators, model outputs, operational decisions, and public claims.
