# Energy Infrastructure Performance Monitoring Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Performance objective | What reliability, degradation, resilience, efficiency, or stewardship problem is monitoring meant to address? | Energy monitoring objective manifest |
| Physical asset layer | Which generation, transmission, distribution, substations, storage, meters, and grid-edge assets are included? | Energy asset inventory |
| Sensing and telemetry | What electrical, thermal, mechanical, environmental, and operational signals are observed? | Performance telemetry record |
| Condition and degradation | How are stress, asset health, aging, cycling, and remaining useful condition represented? | Condition and degradation log |
| Power quality and stability | Are voltage, frequency, harmonics, reactive power, and disturbance indicators monitored? | Power quality and stability records |
| Reliability and resilience | Does the system distinguish routine reliability from stress response, restoration, and fallback capacity? | Reliability and resilience review |
| Cyber-physical integrity | Can telemetry and monitoring systems remain trustworthy during faults, outages, or attacks? | Cybersecurity and telemetry integrity review |
| Governance and action | Are findings connected to maintenance, dispatch, restoration, capital planning, or reporting? | Governance and maintenance action log |
