# Energy Performance Monitoring Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative availability, service continuity, degradation, stress, power-quality risk, and resilience indicators
- flag energy assets requiring governance, inspection, maintenance, operations, or capital-planning review
- support reproducible energy infrastructure performance monitoring examples

## Not intended for

- certified grid operations
- protection engineering automation
- public-safety automation
- regulatory compliance determination
- cybersecurity certification
- replacement for operator judgment

## Required caveats

Sample data and thresholds are illustrative. Real deployment requires validated telemetry, field inspection, asset-specific engineering, protection review, cybersecurity testing, operational authority, and regulatory governance.
