# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before early warning analytics are used for public reporting, emergency operations, warning-system investment, or governance decisions, they should pass a readiness gate.

1. Hazards, exposed communities, critical services, and vulnerable populations are defined.
2. Observation networks, telemetry, maintenance, uptime, and calibration are documented.
3. Forecast products, thresholds, uncertainty, lead time, and impact interpretation are documented.
4. Warning authority, escalation, false-alarm handling, and institutional handoffs are defined.
5. Dissemination channels are redundant, tested, and mapped to exposed populations.
6. Accessibility barriers are reviewed for language, disability access, device access, connectivity, mobility, and trust.
7. Warnings connect to evacuation, sheltering, route closure, anticipatory action, and drills.
8. After-action review, performance indicators, public evidence, and improvement cycles are defined.
