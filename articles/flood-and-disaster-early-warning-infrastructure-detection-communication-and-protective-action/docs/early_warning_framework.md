# Early Warning Infrastructure Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Risk knowledge | Who and what is exposed, vulnerable, and able to act? | Hazard and exposure register |
| Observation and telemetry | Which gauges, stations, radar, satellites, and telemetry systems detect hazards? | Observation network inventory |
| Forecasting and impact interpretation | Which forecast products, thresholds, uncertainty ranges, and lead times support action? | Forecast product register |
| Dissemination and communication | Which channels deliver warnings, with what redundancy and reach? | Warning channel register |
| Inclusion and accessibility | Which barriers affect language, disability access, connectivity, mobility, and trust? | Accessibility and inclusion review |
| Preparedness and response | Which protective actions are triggered, practiced, and supported? | Preparedness action log |
| Governance and learning | Who issues warnings, reviews performance, and corrects failures? | Governance and after-action log |
