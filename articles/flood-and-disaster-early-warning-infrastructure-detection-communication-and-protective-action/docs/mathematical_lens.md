# Mathematical Lens

## Protective warning probability

\[
P_{\mathrm{protective},i} = D_i \times R_i \times C_i \times T_i \times A_i
\]

Protective warning probability depends on detection, reach, comprehension, trust, and feasible action capacity.

## Useful lead time

\[
L^{\mathrm{useful}}_i = L_i - T_{\mathrm{decision},i} - T_{\mathrm{communication},i} - T_{\mathrm{mobilization},i}
\]

Useful lead time is what remains after institutional decision time, communication delay, and mobilization time.

## Residual warning risk

\[
R_{\mathrm{residual},g} = H_g \times E_g \times V_g \times (1 - P_{\mathrm{protective},g})
\]

Residual risk remains high when hazard, exposure, and vulnerability are high and protective warning probability is low.
