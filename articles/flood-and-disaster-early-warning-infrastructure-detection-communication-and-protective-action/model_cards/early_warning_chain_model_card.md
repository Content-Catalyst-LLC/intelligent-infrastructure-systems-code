# Early Warning Chain Model Card

## Intended use

- summarize warning-chain readiness across detection, lead time, dissemination, comprehension, trust, action capacity, accessibility, and governance
- flag zones where high residual warning risk or low useful lead time requires review
- support public evidence, preparedness planning, and warning-system improvement

## Not intended for

- replacing emergency-management judgment
- issuing live warnings
- certifying warning-system safety
- treating a composite score as proof of public protection

## Required caveats

- scores are illustrative
- live warning decisions require operational authority and validated systems
- local knowledge and community feedback are essential
- warning reach does not guarantee comprehension or feasible action
