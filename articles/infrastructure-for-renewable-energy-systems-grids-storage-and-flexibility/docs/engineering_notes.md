# Engineering Notes

## Design priorities

1. Define renewable integration goals before selecting indicators.
2. Preserve asset, grid, storage, flexibility, and governance context.
3. Measure usable renewable energy, curtailment, forecast error, grid constraints, hosting capacity, storage readiness, flexibility adequacy, and resilience.
4. Track data quality, timestamps, latency, missingness, forecast assumptions, resource conditions, and provenance.
5. Distinguish resource variability, equipment underperformance, grid congestion, curtailment, forecast error, and dispatch decisions.
6. Document inverter/device visibility, firmware, cybersecurity, failover, manual operations, and incident response.
7. Connect indicators to grid reinforcement, storage procurement, demand-response design, interconnection reform, permitting action, or public reporting.

## Common failure modes

- installed capacity is treated as usable energy
- curtailment is not linked to grid planning or flexibility action
- storage is deployed without dispatch, degradation, or system-value strategy
- forecast error is not distinguished from asset underperformance
- distributed resources are invisible to planning and operations
- grid-edge devices lack cybersecurity and firmware governance
- public claims are made without auditable evidence chains
