# Renewable Energy Infrastructure Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Renewable objective | What integration, flexibility, resilience, and public-service goals should the infrastructure support? | Renewable infrastructure objective manifest |
| Renewable assets | Which generation, storage, inverter, distributed, and grid-edge assets are included? | Renewable asset inventory |
| Grid connection | Can renewable output be connected, transmitted, and distributed without unacceptable constraint? | Grid connection and constraint register |
| Forecasting and telemetry | Are forecast quality, actual generation, resource conditions, and telemetry integrity tracked? | Renewable generation and forecast records |
| Storage and flexibility | Can the system balance variability across relevant timescales? | Storage and flexibility register |
| Reliability and resilience | Does the system maintain service continuity and recovery capability under stress? | Renewable reliability and resilience review |
| Cyber-physical governance | Are inverters, telemetry, APIs, firmware, and grid-edge devices visible and protected? | Cyber-physical monitoring review |
| Planning and governance | Are findings connected to grid reinforcement, permitting, storage procurement, flexibility markets, or public reporting? | Governance and planning action log |
