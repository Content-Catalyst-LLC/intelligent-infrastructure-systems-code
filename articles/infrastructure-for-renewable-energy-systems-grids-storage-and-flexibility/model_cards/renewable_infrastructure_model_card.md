# Renewable Infrastructure Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative usable renewable energy, curtailment, forecast error, grid constraint, flexibility adequacy, storage readiness, and resilience indicators
- flag renewable assets, grid nodes, and flexibility zones requiring planning, governance, or engineering review
- support reproducible renewable infrastructure examples

## Not intended for

- certified power-system planning
- grid operations automation
- protection engineering
- market settlement
- interconnection approval
- environmental compliance determination
- cybersecurity certification
- replacement for operator or planner judgment

## Required caveats

Sample data and thresholds are illustrative. Real deployment requires validated telemetry, grid models, operator authority, protection review, cybersecurity testing, regulatory governance, environmental review, community engagement, and institution-specific planning judgment.
