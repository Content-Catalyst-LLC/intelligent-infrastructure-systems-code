# Environmental Monitoring Indicator Model Card

## Intended use

- compute illustrative threshold flags, monitoring quality scores, and environmental risk scores
- flag sites where stewardship review may be required
- support reproducible environmental monitoring examples

## Not intended for

- regulatory compliance decisions
- certified environmental assessment
- emergency warning issuance
- replacing field, laboratory, or domain expert review

## Required caveats

- sample data are illustrative
- thresholds are illustrative and must be replaced with authoritative domain-specific thresholds where appropriate
- real monitoring requires calibrated instruments, methods documentation, QA/QC, spatial design review, and public governance
