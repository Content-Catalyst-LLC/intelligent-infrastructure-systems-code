# Edge Environmental Sensor Model Card

## Intended use

- demonstrate edge validation for environmental monitoring telemetry
- detect out-of-range values, low power, and threshold conditions
- compare embedded, MicroPython, and HDL validation patterns

## Not intended for

- certified environmental monitoring
- regulatory enforcement
- replacing calibration or laboratory QA/QC
- emergency warning issuance

## Required caveats

- thresholds are illustrative
- field deployment requires calibration, maintenance, redundancy, metadata capture, and archive integration
