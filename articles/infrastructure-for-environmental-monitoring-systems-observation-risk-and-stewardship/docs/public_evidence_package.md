# Public Evidence Package Template

Required public-facing fields:

- monitoring system name and scope
- environmental domains and variables covered
- sites, platforms, instruments, and operators
- methods, sampling frequency, units, detection limits, and thresholds
- calibration, QA/QC, and quality-flag status
- spatial coverage and known monitoring gaps
- exposure and equity caveats
- data archive version and provenance
- stewardship or response pathway
- governance owner and review cycle
- public reporting schedule

Public-facing evidence should distinguish raw observations, quality-controlled records, indicators, forecasts, and stewardship claims.
