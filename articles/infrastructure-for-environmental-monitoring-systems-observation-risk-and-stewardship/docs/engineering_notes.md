# Engineering Notes

## Design priorities

Environmental monitoring infrastructure should be engineered around the full observation-to-action chain:

1. Define the monitoring question and stewardship use.
2. Observe the relevant variable with suitable field, lab, remote-sensing, or sensor methods.
3. Preserve metadata, calibration, sampling protocol, and provenance.
4. Transmit and archive observations with quality flags.
5. Compute thresholds, anomalies, indicators, and risk-review flags.
6. Review coverage against exposure, vulnerability, and environmental burden.
7. Connect findings to stewardship, enforcement, remediation, adaptation, public health, or public reporting.

## Common failure modes

- sensors deployed without a monitoring objective
- missing site metadata
- undocumented calibration drift
- laboratory methods not linked to observation records
- telemetry gaps treated as zero risk
- indicators published without thresholds or caveats
- spatial averages hiding unequal exposure
- public dashboards not linked to governance action
- monitoring findings not reviewed after environmental events
