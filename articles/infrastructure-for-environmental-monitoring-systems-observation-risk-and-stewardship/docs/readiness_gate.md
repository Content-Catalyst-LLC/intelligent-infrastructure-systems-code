# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before environmental monitoring outputs are used for public reporting, regulatory enforcement, stewardship planning, early warning, exposure analysis, infrastructure-risk assessment, or environmental justice review, they should pass a readiness gate.

1. Domains, variables, indicators, thresholds, and decision uses are defined.
2. Monitoring sites, instruments, coordinates, operators, and status are documented.
3. Sampling design, laboratory methods, sensor methods, detection limits, and units are documented.
4. Calibration, quality flags, range checks, field blanks, laboratory QA/QC, and uncertainty are tracked.
5. Records are standardized, timestamped, versioned, georeferenced, and linked to metadata.
6. Monitoring coverage is reviewed against exposure, vulnerability, and environmental burden.
7. Indicators, baselines, thresholds, caveats, and valid-use limits are documented.
8. Responsible institutions, review cycles, reporting duties, and action pathways are defined.
