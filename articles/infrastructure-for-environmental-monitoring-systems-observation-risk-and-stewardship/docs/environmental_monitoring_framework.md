# Environmental Monitoring Infrastructure Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Monitoring objective | What domain, variable, threshold, or stewardship question is being monitored? | Monitoring objective manifest |
| Observing and sampling | Which field, sensor, laboratory, satellite, or remote-sensing systems produce observations? | Site inventory and sampling protocol |
| Telemetry and transmission | How do observations move from field or platform to data system? | Telemetry and device-health log |
| Metadata and quality | Can measurements be interpreted, compared, and trusted? | Calibration log, quality flags, methods metadata |
| Data integration | Can observations be joined across sites, domains, time, and geography? | SQL schema, archive manifest, GeoJSON layer |
| Indicators and interpretation | How are baselines, thresholds, anomalies, and risk scores constructed? | Threshold and indicator catalog |
| Coverage and equity | Are exposed communities, ecosystems, and high-burden areas monitored? | Coverage and equity review |
| Governance and action | Who responds when monitoring detects concern? | Governance and stewardship log |
