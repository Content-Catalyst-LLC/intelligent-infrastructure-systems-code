# Life-Cycle Stewardship Model Card

## Intended use

- track asset condition, maintenance planning, funded maintenance, renewal planning, and service indicators
- identify maintenance backlog and stewardship gaps
- connect public investment to long-run service obligations

## Not intended for

- replacing engineering asset inspection
- treating maintenance backlog as the only stewardship concern
- ignoring local service realities and fiscal constraints

## Required caveats

- asset condition must be validated by domain experts
- maintenance estimates should be updated regularly
- stewardship is a governance obligation, not only an engineering metric
