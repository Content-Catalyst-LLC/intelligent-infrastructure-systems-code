# Procurement Integrity Model Card

## Intended use

- summarize procurement method, competition status, delivery readiness, variation orders, cost escalation, and delay
- identify procurement records requiring review
- connect contract management to public-value protection

## Not intended for

- replacing legal procurement review
- declaring a procurement fair or unfair without evidence
- using limited indicators as corruption proof

## Required caveats

- procurement findings require documentary review
- competition status should be assessed by sector and market context
- variation orders may be justified but should be traceable
