# Infrastructure Governance Readiness Model Card

## Intended use

- summarize governance readiness across public value, affordability, delivery readiness, stewardship, transparency, accountability, and learning
- flag projects requiring fiscal, procurement, maintenance, disclosure, or institutional review
- support infrastructure governance research, public evidence packages, and comparative workflow demonstrations

## Not intended for

- certifying legal compliance
- replacing professional appraisal
- automating public investment decisions
- ranking communities by worthiness
- treating a composite score as proof of public value

## Required caveats

- scores are illustrative and require validation
- governance findings should be interpreted with institutional context
- public-facing outputs should disclose assumptions and limitations
- affected communities should not be reduced to score inputs
