# Mathematical Lens

Infrastructure governance quality requires more than project approval or completion. It connects public value, affordability, delivery risk, stewardship readiness, accountability, and institutional learning.

## Public value

\[
PV_i =
w_1N_i +
w_2S_i +
w_3A_i +
w_4R_i +
w_5E_i -
w_6D_i
\]

A public-value score combines public need, strategic fit, affordability, resilience, and equity while subtracting delivery risk.

## Life-cycle cost

\[
LCC_i = C^{\mathrm{capital}}_i + \sum_{t=1}^{T}\frac{C^{\mathrm{operations}}_{i,t} + C^{\mathrm{maintenance}}_{i,t} + C^{\mathrm{renewal}}_{i,t}}{(1+r)^t}
\]

Life-cycle cost includes capital cost plus discounted operations, maintenance, and renewal costs over the asset life.

## Delivery risk

\[
DR_i =
\alpha C_i +
\beta K_i +
\gamma P_i +
\delta X_i
\]

Delivery risk combines project complexity, market capacity, procurement risk, and external uncertainty.

## Stewardship quality

\[
Q_{\mathrm{stewardship},i} =
w_1M_i +
w_2O_i +
w_3B_i +
w_4R_i +
w_5V_i
\]

Stewardship quality depends on maintenance planning, operational capability, budget provision, renewal planning, and service visibility.

## Accountability quality

\[
Q_{\mathrm{accountability},i} =
w_1T_i +
w_2C_i +
w_3U_i +
w_4A_i +
w_5L_i
\]

Accountability quality depends on transparency, consultation, usability of disclosure, auditability, and learning.

## Governance quality

\[
GQ_i =
w_1PV_i +
w_2Q_{\mathrm{stewardship},i} +
w_3Q_{\mathrm{accountability},i} +
w_4G_i
\]

Governance quality combines public-value justification, stewardship readiness, accountability quality, and institutional capacity.
