# Infrastructure Governance Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Public purpose | What public-service problem justifies action? | Needs assessment and public-value statement |
| Appraisal and prioritization | Were alternatives compared transparently? | Project appraisal register |
| Fiscal and finance | Is the full life-cycle obligation affordable? | Fiscal-risk and affordability register |
| Procurement and delivery | Can contracts and institutions protect public value under complexity? | Procurement and delivery log |
| Operations and stewardship | Can the asset be maintained, operated, renewed, and adapted over time? | Asset stewardship register |
| Institutional coordination | Who plans, funds, regulates, delivers, operates, audits, and communicates? | Responsibility matrix |
| Transparency and accountability | Can decisions and outcomes be scrutinized? | Public evidence package |
| Learning and adaptation | Are failures translated into revised standards, budgets, and institutional design? | Policy learning record |
