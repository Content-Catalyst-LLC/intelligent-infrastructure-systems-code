# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before infrastructure governance analytics, project scorecards, procurement dashboards, asset stewardship reports, or public evidence systems are used for decision-making, they should pass a readiness gate.

## Checks

1. Public-service scope, affected publics, decision use, and valid-use limits are defined.
2. Projects have appraisal records that document alternatives, public-value rationale, and rejected options.
3. Capital cost, operations, maintenance, renewal, and fiscal-risk records are present.
4. Procurement method, competition status, contract model, variation orders, and delivery risks are documented.
5. Asset condition, maintenance backlog, service indicators, and renewal needs are tracked.
6. Institutional ownership is documented for planning, funding, delivery, operation, regulation, audit, and public communication.
7. Transparency, consultation, auditability, and disclosure usability are assessed.
8. Scores and claims can be traced back to data sources, assumptions, dates, and responsible institutions.
9. Public-facing outputs are intelligible and avoid decorative transparency.
10. Findings connect to policy learning, standards updates, budget decisions, procurement guidance, or institutional reform.
