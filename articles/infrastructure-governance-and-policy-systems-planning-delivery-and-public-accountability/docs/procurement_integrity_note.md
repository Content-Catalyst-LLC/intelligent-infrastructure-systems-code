# Procurement Integrity Note

Procurement integrity requires more than formal tender compliance.

Review whether:

- needs and specifications were defined before tendering
- alternatives were considered
- competition was adequate
- evaluation criteria were published and applied consistently
- conflicts of interest were declared
- risk allocation was realistic
- contract variations are justified and disclosed
- performance monitoring is active after award
- complaints and audit findings have a response pathway

The contract award is not the end of governance. It is the start of delivery oversight.
