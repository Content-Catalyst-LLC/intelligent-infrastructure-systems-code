# Adaptation Readiness Model Card

## Intended use

- summarize climate adaptation readiness across exposed infrastructure systems
- flag high-criticality systems with high residual risk or low implementation readiness
- support governance reporting, prioritization, and public evidence review

## Not intended for

- certifying infrastructure safety
- replacing engineering design review
- automating public-investment decisions
- treating composite scores as definitive risk estimates
- hiding uncertainty, political choice, or community knowledge

## Required caveats

- scores are illustrative
- climate projections require local review
- adaptation effectiveness requires engineering and ecological validation
- vulnerability indicators should be interpreted with community context
- public-facing outputs should avoid false precision
