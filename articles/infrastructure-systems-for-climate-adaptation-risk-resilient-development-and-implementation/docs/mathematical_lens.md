# Mathematical Lens

Infrastructure adaptation should distinguish hazard, exposure, sensitivity, adaptive capacity, adaptation option effectiveness, residual risk, and implementation readiness.

\[
R_i = H_i \times E_i \times S_i \times (1 - AC_i)
\]

Climate risk for system \(i\) increases with hazard intensity, exposure, and sensitivity, and decreases as adaptive capacity improves.

\[
R^{\mathrm{residual}}_i = R_i(1 - O_i) + M_i
\]

Residual risk remains after adaptation options are applied. Maladaptation can increase risk even if local hazard exposure falls.

\[
Q_{\mathrm{adaptation}} = w_1C + w_2A + w_3V + w_4F + w_5I + w_6M + w_7G
\]

Adaptation quality combines climate evidence, asset visibility, vulnerability analysis, finance, implementation, monitoring, and governance.
