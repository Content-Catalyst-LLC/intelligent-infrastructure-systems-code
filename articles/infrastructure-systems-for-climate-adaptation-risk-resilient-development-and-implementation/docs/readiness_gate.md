# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before adaptation analytics are used for public reporting, infrastructure prioritization, standards revision, or investment decisions, they should pass a readiness gate.

1. Public-service scope, infrastructure boundary, hazards, and valid-use limits are defined.
2. Climate hazards, time horizons, assumptions, uncertainty, and data sources are documented.
3. Infrastructure assets, criticality, condition, service roles, and dependencies are inventoried.
4. Vulnerability, affordability, adaptive capacity, and community priorities are assessed.
5. Adaptation options include engineered, ecological, operational, standards-based, demand-management, and relocation pathways where relevant.
6. Maladaptation risk is screened.
7. Funding, procurement, delivery status, maintenance, and operations readiness are documented.
8. Monitoring, residual-risk review, public evidence, and governance learning cycles are defined.
