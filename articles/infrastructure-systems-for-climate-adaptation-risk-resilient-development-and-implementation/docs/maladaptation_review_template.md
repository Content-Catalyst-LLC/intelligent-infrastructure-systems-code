# Maladaptation Review Template

- Does the intervention reduce risk for one group while increasing risk elsewhere?
- Does it lock development into areas that may become unsafe or unaffordable to defend?
- Does it damage ecological systems that provide adaptive protection?
- Does it increase emissions, energy demand, water stress, or operating burden?
- Does it privilege high-value assets over vulnerable populations?
- Does it preserve future options or narrow them?
- Does it create a false sense of resilience without improving service continuity?
