# Cyber Resilience Readiness Model Card

## Intended use

- summarize infrastructure cyber resilience across asset visibility, identity governance, control effectiveness, detection, containment, recovery, continuity, and governance
- flag systems requiring continuity, recovery, identity, detection, residual exposure, or governance review
- support public-service cyber-resilience reporting

## Not intended for

- certifying compliance
- replacing formal security assessment
- exposing sensitive infrastructure details
- automating public safety decisions
- treating a composite score as a guarantee of resilience

## Required caveats

- scores are illustrative
- control effectiveness requires validation
- OT and ICS findings require engineering review
- public-facing outputs should avoid sensitive implementation details
