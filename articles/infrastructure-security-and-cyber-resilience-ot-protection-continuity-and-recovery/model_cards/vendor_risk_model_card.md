# Vendor Risk Model Card

## Intended use

- document vendors, remote access, managed services, cloud dependencies, software suppliers, contract controls, and concentration risk
- support third-party risk review
- connect procurement and contract management to cyber resilience

## Not intended for

- replacing legal contract review
- revealing sensitive supplier details publicly
- assuming contractual transfer equals operational resilience

## Required caveats

- vendor access requires ongoing review
- concentration risk should be assessed at service level
- supplier compromise can become public-service disruption
