# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before infrastructure security and cyber-resilience systems are used for public reporting, operational assurance, incident response, OT modernization, digital infrastructure procurement, critical-service continuity planning, or governance decisions, they should pass a readiness gate.

## Checks

1. Public-service scope, cyber-physical boundary, owners, and valid-use limits are defined.
2. IT, OT, field devices, software, identities, remote access, vendors, and data flows are inventoried.
3. Privileged users, service accounts, vendor access, and remote access are governed.
4. IT, OT, public, vendor, and safety-relevant zones are separated and tested.
5. Cyber and operational anomalies can be detected and triaged.
6. Teams can contain compromise without creating unsafe process consequences.
7. Systems, data, operations, and public services can be restored safely.
8. Essential functions can continue under degraded cyber-physical conditions.
9. Third-party access, software dependence, contracts, and concentration risk are governed.
10. Risk ownership, funding, public communication, exception approval, and after-action learning are defined.
