# Cyber-Resilience Note

Future intelligent infrastructure increases the importance of cyber-physical resilience.

A cyber-resilience package should document:

- segmentation and isolation
- identity and access controls
- monitoring and detection
- backup and recovery
- manual fallback capability
- incident-response plan
- tabletop or exercise record
- dependency and platform risk
- public-service continuity assumptions
- governance owner

Intelligent infrastructure should be judged by how it behaves when connectivity, data, automation, or platforms degrade.
