# AI Governance Note

AI systems in intelligent infrastructure should be treated as high-consequence decision-support systems.

Required governance elements:

- model purpose
- model owner
- training or calibration source
- decision-use boundary
- validation status
- drift monitoring
- false-positive and false-negative review
- bias and equity review
- human authority and override
- incident and escalation log
- public evidence caveat

AI should improve visibility, triage, forecasting, and coordination without replacing accountable institutional judgment.
