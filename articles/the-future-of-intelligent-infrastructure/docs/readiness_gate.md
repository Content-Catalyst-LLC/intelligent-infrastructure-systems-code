# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before an intelligent infrastructure system is used for operations, public reporting, AI-assisted triage, infrastructure planning, predictive maintenance, emergency response, cyber-physical coordination, digital public services, or climate adaptation decisions, it should pass a readiness gate.

## Checks

1. Public-service purpose, decision use, responsible owner, and valid-use limits are defined.
2. Critical assets, dependencies, service outcomes, and failure modes are observable.
3. Data sources are documented, current, quality-checked, interoperable, and governed.
4. AI and analytics models are documented, validated, monitored, and connected to human review.
5. Cyber-resilience controls include segmentation, access control, isolation, recovery, and incident response.
6. Climate, demand, outage, cyber, supply-chain, and cascading-failure scenarios are documented.
7. Equity, service quality, monitoring coverage, and risk reduction are evaluated across communities.
8. Accountability, decision rights, review processes, public communication, and update responsibilities are defined.
9. Insights can be converted into maintenance, operations, emergency response, planning, or public decisions.
