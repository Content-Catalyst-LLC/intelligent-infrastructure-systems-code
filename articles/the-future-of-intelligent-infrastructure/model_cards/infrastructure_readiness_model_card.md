# Infrastructure Intelligence Readiness Model Card

## Intended use

- summarize intelligent infrastructure readiness across observability, interoperability, AI governance, resilience, cyber resilience, equity, public accountability, and adaptive capacity
- identify systems requiring governance review
- support public-value and resilience reporting
- create reproducible article companion outputs

## Not intended for

- replacing engineering inspection
- certifying regulatory compliance
- automating public infrastructure decisions
- ranking communities without contextual review
- hiding uncertainty behind a composite score

## Required caveats

- scores are illustrative
- dimensions require local calibration
- public value cannot be reduced to a single number
- governance review is required for high-consequence use
