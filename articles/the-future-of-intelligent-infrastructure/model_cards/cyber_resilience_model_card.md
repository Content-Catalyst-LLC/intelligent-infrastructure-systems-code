# Cyber-Resilience Model Card

## Intended use

- assess segmentation, access control, recovery, monitoring, incident response, and continuity readiness
- identify high-criticality systems requiring cyber-resilience review
- connect cyber posture to public-service continuity

## Not intended for

- replacing formal cybersecurity assessment
- certifying compliance
- public disclosure of sensitive cyber details

## Required caveats

- public outputs should avoid sensitive control details
- operational technology risks require specialized review
- cyber resilience must include recovery and fallback, not only prevention
