# AI Governance Model Card

## Intended use

- document infrastructure AI systems used for anomaly detection, forecasting, predictive maintenance, optimization, inspection, and triage
- record validation status, human review, and decision-use limits
- support accountable public infrastructure analytics

## Not intended for

- fully autonomous critical infrastructure control
- decisions without human authority
- hiding value judgments behind technical recommendations
- deployment without drift monitoring and review

## Required caveats

- AI outputs are decision-support inputs
- model drift and false classifications require monitoring
- equity and public accountability review are necessary for public-service use
