# Lifecycle Cost Model Card

## Intended use

- compare maintain, repair, rehabilitate, renew, replace, or defer options
- make long-term cost consequences visible
- include maintenance, operations, capital renewal, and expected failure or disruption costs
- support budget and renewal planning

## Not intended for

- replacing full engineering economic analysis
- hiding uncertainty in cost, demand, climate exposure, or deterioration
- justifying short-term deferral without risk acceptance

## Required caveats

- costs are scenario-dependent
- discount rates affect conclusions
- avoided failure costs are uncertain
- service, safety, equity, and environmental consequences may exceed monetized estimates
