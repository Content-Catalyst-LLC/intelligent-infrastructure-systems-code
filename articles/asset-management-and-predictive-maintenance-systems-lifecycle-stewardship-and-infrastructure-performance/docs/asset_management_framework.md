# Asset Management and Predictive Maintenance Framework

| Layer | Question | Evidence artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Asset knowledge | What assets exist, where are they, and who owns responsibility? | Asset register and component hierarchy |
| Condition and performance | What is the asset condition and service performance? | Inspection records, telemetry, defects, downtime |
| Criticality | What happens if this asset fails? | Criticality and consequence register |
| Maintenance strategy | Should intervention be reactive, preventive, condition-based, predictive, or renewal-oriented? | Maintenance strategy policy |
| Predictive insight | What indicators provide actionable lead time before failure? | Predictive maintenance model card |
| Lifecycle cost | What are the whole-life costs of maintain, repair, renew, replace, or defer? | Lifecycle cost scenario table |
| Work execution | Can the institution act on the priority? | Work orders, parts, outage windows, workforce records |
| Governance | Who accepts risk, funds intervention, and reviews outcomes? | Governance review log |
