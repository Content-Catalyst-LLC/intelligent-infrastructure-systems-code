# Deployment Readiness Gate

Before an asset management or predictive maintenance system is used for operational prioritization, lifecycle planning, risk review, capital renewal, public performance reporting, or budget justification, it should pass a readiness gate.

## Checks

1. Asset register includes asset IDs, class, owner, location, age, hierarchy, and service role.
2. Condition records are current enough for the decision being made.
3. Criticality and consequence scoring are documented and reviewed.
4. Failure probability or risk model is transparent, validated, or explicitly caveated.
5. Maintenance strategy logic distinguishes reactive, preventive, condition-based, predictive, and renewal actions.
6. Remaining useful life estimates include uncertainty and valid-use limits.
7. Lifecycle cost scenarios include maintenance, operations, capital renewal, disruption, and failure consequence.
8. Predictive outputs connect to work orders, budget, workforce, parts, and outage coordination.
9. Governance records identify who reviewed, funded, accepted, deferred, or escalated intervention.
